Word list

Ecology words

EcologyThe study of the relationships between living things and their environment. Living together.
BioticLiving. Flowers, cats
AbioticNon-living. Rocks, water
IndividualA single member of a species. A horse, a bee
SpeciesLiving things similar enough to produce offspring together. Male & female horses
HabitatA natural environment with the biotic and abiotic resources a species needs to survive. Log, swamp, ocean, Arctic
PopulationHow many of a species live in an area. Always a number. 4 sharks
CommunityAll the living things in an area. Coral reef: sharks, stingrays, fish, crabs
EcosystemThe system of interactions between living things and their environment. Pond, forest, desert, wetland
BiodiversityA wide variety of living things. Rainforest, coral reef
BiomeA large region of similar ecosystems because of a distinct climate. Arctic tundra, savannah, boreal forest

The four spheres

AtmosphereAir: layers of gas
Nitrogen 78%
Oxygen 21%
LithosphereRock: Earth's rocky surface
Mountains, ocean floor, continents
HydrosphereWater: ALL of it
Oceans, lakes, ice, groundwater, clouds
BiosphereLife: where living things are
Rainforests, ocean trenches, mountaintops
